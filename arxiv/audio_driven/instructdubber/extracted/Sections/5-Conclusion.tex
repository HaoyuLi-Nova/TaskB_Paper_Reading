


\section{Conclusion}
In this paper, we propose InstructDubber, a novel instruction-based alignment dubbing method that achieves robust both in-domain and zero-shot dubbing.
% The proposed instructed duration distilling module and the instructed emotion calibrating module facilitate the instruction-based duration and prosody prediction.
The proposed instructed duration distilling module and the instructed emotion calibrating effectively leverage fine-grained dubbing instructions to facilitate temporal and emotional alignment, respectively.
The superior performance on both in-domain and zero-shot experiments across three major benchmarks, along with the ablation studies, demonstrates the effectiveness of the proposed method and each module.
